Choosing between cloud AI and on-device AI

AI Nova 2026-09-06

What to consider when deciding where an AI feature should actually run.

Cloud-based AI offers more processing power and access to larger models, making it suitable for complex tasks. On-device AI, on the other hand, works offline, responds faster and keeps data local, which matters for privacy-sensitive use cases.

The right choice depends on the task: quick, private, everyday interactions often work well on-device, while research-heavy or highly complex requests usually benefit from cloud processing.
