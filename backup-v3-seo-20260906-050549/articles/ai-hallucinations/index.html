Understanding AI hallucinations and how to reduce them

AI Nova 2026-09-06

Why AI models sometimes generate confident but incorrect answers, and practical ways to catch it.

AI hallucinations happen when a model generates information that sounds plausible but is factually wrong. This is not a bug in the traditional sense — it is a natural consequence of how language models predict text based on patterns rather than verified facts.

To reduce the impact of hallucinations, always verify specific facts, dates and figures against a reliable source before relying on them. Asking the model to cite sources, breaking complex questions into smaller steps, and cross-checking critical outputs are simple habits that significantly improve reliability.
