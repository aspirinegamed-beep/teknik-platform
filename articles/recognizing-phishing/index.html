Recognizing phishing attempts in emails and messages

Common warning signs that reveal a fraudulent message before it's too late.

Phishing messages often create urgency, asking you to act immediately to avoid a penalty or claim a reward. Legitimate organizations rarely pressure users into instant action through email.

Checking the actual sender address rather than the display name, hovering over links before clicking, and never entering credentials through a link received by email are simple habits that block the vast majority of phishing attempts.
