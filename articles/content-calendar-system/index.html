A simple content calendar system for consistent publishing

AI Nova 2026-09-06

A lightweight planning method that keeps publishing steady without burnout.

Consistency matters more than volume. A simple calendar with one column for topics, one for status and one for publish date is enough to keep a small content operation organized without heavy tools.

Batching similar tasks — research one week, writing the next, editing after that — reduces context-switching and makes it realistic to sustain a publishing schedule over the long term.
