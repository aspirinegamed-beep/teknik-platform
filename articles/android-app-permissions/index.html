Setting up secure app permissions on Android

A practical approach to reviewing and managing what apps can access.

Every permission an app requests should match its core function. A flashlight app asking for contacts access, for example, is a clear warning sign that deserves attention before granting anything.

Android allows reviewing and revoking permissions at any time from Settings. Regularly auditing location, microphone, camera and contacts access — especially for apps that are rarely used — is one of the simplest ways to reduce unnecessary data exposure.
