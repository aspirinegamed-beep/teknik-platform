Best practices for organizing Android home screens

A cleaner layout that reduces distraction and speeds up daily use.

An overcrowded home screen slows you down and increases distraction. Keeping only the four or five most-used apps visible, with everything else organized into folders or an app drawer, creates a calmer and faster experience.

Grouping apps by purpose — communication, productivity, media — rather than by app name, makes it easier to find what you need quickly, especially on devices with smaller screens.
