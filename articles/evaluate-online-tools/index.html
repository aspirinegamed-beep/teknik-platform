How to evaluate any online tool before adopting it

A short checklist to avoid switching tools every few months.

Before adopting a new tool, define the specific problem it needs to solve and check whether it actually solves that problem better than your current process — not just whether it looks appealing.

Check data export options, pricing changes over time, and how actively the tool is maintained. A tool that is hard to leave later is a bigger risk than one that simply lacks a feature today.
